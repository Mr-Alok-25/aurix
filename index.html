BackupSave your favourites and playlists to a file.
UpdatesThe app tells you when a new version is ready.

How to install

  1. Tap Download and wait for the file aurix.apk.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps (Settings > Install unknown apps).
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, choose Install anyway.

Questions

Why does Android show a warning?

AURIX is not on the Play Store, so Android shows its normal warning for apps installed from a file.

How do I update?

Open the app. If a new version is out, it asks you to update. Tap Update, then open the downloaded file. Your favourites and playlists stay.

What does the app collect?

Only anonymous usage counts (screens opened, minutes of music). It never records song names or what you search. You can turn it off in Profile > Privacy & Security.
